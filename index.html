What happens after you submit

Your complaint moves through four stages — Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed — and every stage change is logged with a date and a note from the staff member handling it.

Your information stays confidential

Complaints are visible only to staff on a need-to-know basis, in line with our data protection obligations and the sensitivity of civil service and defence force records.
